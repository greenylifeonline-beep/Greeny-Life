# MasterMind AI Agents

MasterMind is a read-only decision orchestrator, not a commercial entity or autonomous executor.

| Agent | Purpose | May not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence & Compliance | Build a fail-closed evidence picture | Assert missing evidence or file customs documents |
| Product & Market | Read canonical portfolio and internal market signals | Invent demand, prices, or market authorization |
| Trade Corridor | Assess company-to-company requirements | Create a shipment, invoice, payment, or title transfer |
| Traceability | Resolve historical or current batch lineage | Treat historical status as current certification |
| System Learning | Identify reusable assets and improvement candidates | Modify code, policies, data, or models automatically |

`POST /api/mastermind/decision-package` combines these agents into one auditable package. Its response always has `automaticExecution: false`.
