## Laws

2. Ready and almost suitable → adapt the keeper, then delete the duplicate.
3. Unsuitable or byte-identical barn → delete immediately.
4. Presence is not proof. Path-signal is not capability. Printed PASS is not truth.
5. Do not create `migration/gl-004` or `migration/gl-005` to pad Wave 2.
6. Do not recreate retired worktrees.
7. Do not start a new registry, bus, or archive forest.
