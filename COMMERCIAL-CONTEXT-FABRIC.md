# Commercial Context Fabric

MasterMind AI reads the canonical customer, demand, and opportunity records as one internal commercial context. It attaches a customer only by canonical ID, verifies the requested product and destination against that context, and recommends the operating company for GCC and EU markets.

It does not treat internal records as external compliance proof or as authorization to make an offer, sign a contract, ship, invoice, pay, file customs documents, or transfer title. Any mismatch or missing ownership assignment remains under MasterMind review and requires explicit user approval.
