# Three-Company Trade Governance

Status: **proposed operating policy pending legal-entity and authority approval**.

| System / company | Authority | May not do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MasterMind AI | Intelligence, verification, recommendations, governance | Be commercial counterparty, pay, file customs, or transfer title |
| Greeny-Life Egypt | Production, suppliers, Egyptian origin/export preparation | Destination import clearance or payment execution |
| Greens Nature | UAE/GCC import, distribution, settlement/re-export review | Egypt origin authority or Norway/EU compliance authority |
| Green Lines | Norway/EU sourcing, import/compliance, distribution | Egypt origin authority or payment execution |

Every movement between Greeny-Life Egypt, Greens Nature, and Green Lines is a reviewed corridor. A corridor never automatically executes shipment, invoice, customs filing, payment, or title transfer.
