# Greeny-Life Egypt Brain

This is the first of the three operating brains. It uses existing canonical assets rather than recreating them: Egyptian product master, supplier master, certificate catalog, warehouses, stock, and shipment records.

It is a read-only operational intelligence layer. It can identify operational context, low-stock conditions, unapproved suppliers, shipment records, and internal opportunities. It does not claim that historical records are live proof. It escalates cross-company trade, new markets, exceptions, material commercial changes, and compliance gaps to MasterMind AI. No commercial execution occurs without explicit user approval.
