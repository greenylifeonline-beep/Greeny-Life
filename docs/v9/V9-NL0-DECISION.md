# RAIOS V9.NL-0 — NeuroLingua Semantic Kernel

## Core decision

Model is replaceable. Meaning is canonical.

## Initial language scope

- ar-EG
- ar-GULF
- en
- nb-NO
- sv-SE
- da-DK

## Execution strategy

Text-first.

The semantic kernel must not depend on a specific LLM vendor or model.

Facts belong in retrieval/semantic memory.

Repeated procedures should become compiled skills.

Persistent behavioral gaps may become adapter candidates.

Training is not the default response to knowledge gaps.

QLoRA/LoRA are the first adapter candidates only after benchmark evidence.

MoRA, MoE-LoRA and continual pretraining remain experimental escalation paths.

## Durability

Linguistic learning events append to local Cognitive WAL first.

Remote replication must remain asynchronous.

No remote ACK may block fast learning.

## Safety

HIGH and CRITICAL transformations require stronger semantic verification.

Canonical promotion remains governed separately from fast discovery.
